## Why did I make it?

After spending almost 4 years developing [Cadaver](/portfolio/cadaver) in 2D, I decided I wanted to challenge myself by writing a 3D project. These prototypes served as my introduction to writing a 3D framework with C++ and OpenGL. 

## What have I implemented?

In the project I implemented:

* Kinematics for a 3D player controller, allowing you to look around and jump between platforms
* AABB Collisions ensuring proper collisions with platforms
* Bill board sprites for enemies which rotate on the Y-axis to always face the player using GLM
* Rather than drawing each plane on a map, I used draw call batching to group all their vertices into a single VBO and render it one draw call

## What are my plans going forward?

This project did its indended goal of helping me learning about programming a game in 3D. Once I succesfully implemented some cubes and the player controller, I realized I wouldn't be satisifed until I knew the math behind what I was doing.
